1 · In one minute

  • P&C (another team) says who a party is. We keep only its id.
  • T12 opens the customer's account with us: category, receivable ledger, credit terms.
  • T24: Billing raises the invoice. The Accounts Executive books its accounting voucher: 3 tables (voucher, lines, history), the twin of Manoj's purchase voucher. Rajan approves. The entry posts.
  • The books core (Manoj, built) holds the posted journal: journal_entry + journal_line, shared by purchase and sales. What a customer owes is read from it.

2 · Manoj's model, and ours

  PURCHASE (Manoj, built)                    SALES (T24, ours)
  purchase_invoice ─┬─ purchase_invoice_line   sales_invoice ─┬─ sales_invoice_line
                    └─ purchase_invoice_history               └─ sales_invoice_history
            │ approve → PostJournalEntry                │ approve → PostJournalEntry
            └──────────────►  journal_entry ─ journal_line  ◄──────────────┘
                              (one shared book: trial balance, what a vendor / customer owes)
Manoj's tableOursSame, and the differences
fin.purchase_invoice (27)fin.sales_invoice (27)The voucher. Same columns; vendor → customer; supplier_invoice_no → invoice_no (Billing's). His source_extraction_id (the uploaded PDF) is not needed: Billing hands the invoice over. Rev 15: + costing_method (FIFO or weighted average, the method its cost lines used).
fin.purchase_invoice_line (9)fin.sales_invoice_line (11)Side, a ledger OR the party, amount. His vendor line must be Cr; our customer line must be Dr. Rev 15: his inventory_item_code is here too (stock comes in on his, goes out on ours), + quantity and stock_lot_id (FIFO: which lot the cost came from).
—inv.stock_lot (12)Rev 15, a stub of inventory (not accounting): the lots and their cost. The cost of goods sold is read from it.
fin.purchase_invoice_history (10)fin.sales_invoice_history (10)Identical: one row per version (submit, decide, amended).
fin.journal_entry + fin.journal_linethe same two tablesShared. The only change: voucher_type also allows sales.
fin.branch, fiscal_year, document_sequencethe sameShared. A new number series row: sales_invoice (SV-0001).
fin.supplier_invoice_extraction—His upload of the supplier's PDF. Sales has no upload: Billing's invoice arrives already typed.
  • No GST columns, as his: GST is just lines on the Output CGST / SGST / IGST ledgers (the ledger's account_type says which).
  • No rule table, as his: the rules R1–R12 propose the lines in code; the AE may pick another ledger; nothing about the proposal is stored.
  • Created at Submit, as his: the voucher row exists once Teja sends it. Billing's invoices waiting to be booked are Billing's list (G37).
  • A correction, as his: a new row SV-0012-1 that amends the posted one; approving it reverses the old entry and posts the new one.
  • Rev 15: the cost of goods sold, in the same entry. The invoice gives the item and quantity; inventory (inv.stock_lot) gives the cost; the method (FIFO or weighted average) decides which. Dr Cost of Goods Sold · Cr Finished Goods. Stock goes down at approval, in the same transaction.
