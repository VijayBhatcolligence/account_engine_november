Proposal 1 · M2 Tally migration · 15 Oct 2026

Who creates the operated company?

Today: the Tally connector creates the company (legal_entity) and its PAN/GSTIN from the Tally book.

Proposal: AIBD collects the company's details at onboarding, the client accepts them, and they are stored once in Parties & Contacts. Accounting refers to the company by its party_id. The Tally connector only checks each book against it.

Company names, PANs and GSTINs here are examples, not real data.
